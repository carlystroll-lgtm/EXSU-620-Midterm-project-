## Interpretation

These are repeated samples at each held position, not ten independent repositioning trials. The missing independent 0° trial was subsequently collected on November 8, after repositioning, with +1.50° mean roll. It is identified as a later capture; the original post-correction zero check remains calibration evidence.

An overall accuracy pass has not been established. The latest −60° trial 1 repeat is within tolerance (+1.66° error); its earlier −6.94° error remains in the evidence and its cause is unresolved. The later +60° trial 2 repeat is also within tolerance (+1.24° error). Every latest selected angle/trial capture is now within tolerance, but earlier out-of-tolerance attempts remain part of the evidence; the cause of those discrepancies has not been established. An exclusion requires a documented setup error, not merely an improved repeat result.
